Overview

What is Thymalin?

Thymalin is a polypeptide complex isolated from calf thymus, whose characterised active components include the dipeptides Lys-Glu and Glu-Trp. It is used as a research compound in studies of T-cell differentiation, hematopoietic stem cell markers, and immune senescence. Supplied for in-vitro and laboratory research use only.

Also known asThymic polypeptide complexThymalinumKE and EW dipeptide complex
