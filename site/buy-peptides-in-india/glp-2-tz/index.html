Overview

What is GLP-2 (TZ)?

GLP-2 (TZ) is a synthetic dual incretin receptor agonist that activates both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors from a single peptide. It is used as a research compound in studies of incretin signaling, glucose regulation, and body composition. Supplied for in-vitro and laboratory research use only.
