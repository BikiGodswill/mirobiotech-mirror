Careers

Join the team advancing peptide research worldwide.

Why Miro Biotech

Miro Biotech develops and supplies research-grade peptides to labs and independent researchers in 60+ countries. We also develop novel peptide sequences in-house, compounds you can't find anywhere else. Small team, profitable, growing fast. Most roles here are ground-floor hires, which means you build the playbook from scratch.

View Open Roles

What We Value

The principles that guide how we work, what we build, and who we hire.

Scientific Rigor

Every decision runs through the data. We test, document, and verify before anything ships. The researchers who depend on us hold themselves to that standard, and so do we.

Global Impact

Our peptides reach labs in 60+ countries. The work here directly supports published research across metabolic, regenerative, and cognitive pathways.

Radical Transparency

Open certificates of analysis, third-party testing results, and honest communication. We earn trust through verifiable evidence, not marketing claims.

Ownership & Speed

Small team, real ownership. Every person here has the autonomy to make decisions and the accountability to see them through.

What You'll Work On

We're building the infrastructure that makes peptide research more reliable, accessible, and transparent.

Peptide R&D

Design and evaluate novel peptide sequences. Run stability, solubility, and activity assessments on new candidates before they go to market.

Quality & Testing

Review third-party HPLC and mass spec data for every batch we release. Write certificates of analysis and build out QC documentation as we scale.

Global Supply Chain

Coordinate international shipments with cold-chain requirements across 60+ countries. Manage supplier relationships and optimize fulfillment.

Platform Engineering

Own the full stack of our Next.js e-commerce site, from product pages to checkout. Build internal tools for order management, inventory, and batch documentation.

Growth Marketing

Own SEO from keyword research through content creation. Figure out where researchers spend time online and get us in front of them.

Research Support

Serve as the scientific resource for our clients, advising on reconstitution protocols, storage conditions, and experimental design.

Benefits & Culture

We take care of our team so they can focus on doing their best work.

Remote-First

Work from anywhere. We hire globally and collaborate asynchronously across time zones.

Salary + Equity

99th percentile salary with meaningful equity for full-time roles. You own a piece of what you build.

Research Budget

Annual stipend for conferences, courses, and publications. We invest in your professional development.

Flexible Schedule

Results over hours. Structure your day around your life, not the other way around.

Health & Wellness

Very generous monthly health stipend to cover insurance, gym, or whatever keeps you at your best. Same benefit everywhere, no matter where you live.

Ground-Floor Roles

No layers of management. You work alongside the people who use what you build and ship accordingly.

Ready to Make an Impact?

We're profitable, growing, and hiring across research, engineering, and marketing. If you want to build something real with a small team that ships fast, we want to hear from you.
